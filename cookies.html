Chocolate Chips Cookie

Welcome to the ultimate guide for making mini chocolate chip cookies! These bite-sized treats are perfect for satisfying your sweet tooth without overindulging. Follow this simple recipe to create delicious, crispy-on-the-outside, chewy-on-the-inside mini chocolate chip cookies that everyone will love.

Ingredients

  • Butter: 1 cup (2 sticks), softened or melted
  • Granulated White Sugar: 3/4 cup
  • Packed Brown Sugar: 3/4 cup (light or dark)
  • Eggs: 2 large eggs
  • Vanilla Extract: 1 to 2 teaspoons for flavor
  • All-Purpose Flour: 2 1/4 cups
  • Baking Soda: 1 teaspoon (helps the cookies rise)
  • Salt: 1/2 to 1 teaspoon (balances the sweetness)
  • Chocolate Chips: 2 cups (semi-sweet or milk chocolate)

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix the flour, baking soda, and salt in a separate bowl.
  3. Beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together until smooth.
  4. Add the eggs and vanilla extract to the butter mixture, then beat until well blended.
  5. Gradually stir the dry flour mixture into the wet ingredients until combined.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips gently.
  7. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto an ungreased baking sheet.
  8. Bake for 9 to 12 minutes until the edges are golden brown.
  9. Cool the cookies on the baking sheet before moving them to a wire rack.
